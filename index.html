Patakla! – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Developer: SerdarGames

Patakla! is a single-player comedy beat 'em up for iPhone in which Kebapçı Usta and his friends chase cartoon aliens through seven stages in Türkiye. It is made by SerdarGames, an individual developer who appears as the seller on the App Store ("we", "us"). The game has no accounts and no servers of its own, so most of this page is about the two outside services built into it: Google's ads and Unity's purchase library. Türkçe metin aşağıdadır.

In short

  • You play without signing in. The game never asks for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or GPS location.
  • Your progress is a file on your iPhone. Nothing from it reaches us: we have no server to receive it.
  • Google AdMob shows the ads, and Google's consent tool asks for your ad choices. Both collect device and usage data (see Google AdMob).
  • Apple takes the payment for NO ADS and teeth packs. Unity In-App Purchasing, the library that talks to the App Store, reports purchase events with device details to Unity (see Unity In-App Purchasing).
  • The choices are yours: ad consent in the game, tracking in iOS Settings, and NO ADS to remove full-screen ads (see Your controls).
  • We do not sell personal information for money.

What stays on your iPhone

The game keeps one small save file in its own storage on the phone. It holds:

  • settings: sound, music, vibration, the green goo effect and the language;
  • progress: unlocked stages, stars, best scores, Horde Mode records, your teeth (the in-game currency), unlocked heroes, buddies, weapon upgrades and finishes, and daily reward and daily mission progress;
  • purchase and ad bookkeeping: whether you own NO ADS, the IDs of purchases already credited (so a teeth pack is never added twice) and a few ad counters, for example when the last full-screen ad was shown.

Google's consent tool also keeps your consent choice on the phone. Daily rewards use only the phone's clock. The iOS app sandbox protects the file, and deleting the app deletes it. NO ADS can be restored after a reinstall; teeth, like the rest of your progress, exist only in this file.

Google AdMob (ads) and Google's consent tool

Ads appear in two ways:

  • Full-screen ads can come up after the results screen of a stage or a Horde Mode run, as you move on. They never interrupt play, never appear before you have cleared Galata Sahili and Bebek Sahili (the first two stages), and come at most once every few minutes.
  • Rewarded ads play only when you ask for one: to get back up after a knockout (once per run), or to play one run with a hero you have not unlocked yet. You can always decline. If no ad can be loaded, for example offline, you get the reward anyway.

To serve ads, cap how often they show, measure them, fight fraud and run analytics, Google's software in the app collects:

  • Device identifiers: Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks; otherwise identifiers limited to this app or developer (such as the identifier for vendors, IDFV) and Google's own app-scoped identifiers.
  • IP address and the approximate (coarse) location worked out from it, such as country or region. Google's consent tool uses it as well, to decide which privacy rules apply to you. The game never accesses GPS location.
  • Ad activity: ads requested and shown, taps on ads, rewarded ads watched to the end, and app launches.
  • Device and app details: phone model, iOS version, language and app version.
  • Diagnostics: crash and performance data from the ad software.

You see personalised ads only where you have given the required consent and allowed tracking. Otherwise Google may show non-personalised or limited ads chosen by context and approximate location. The game limits ads to content rated PG or lower.

With NO ADS there are no full-screen ads, and the revive and the hero try-out come without an ad (still once per run). From the next launch on, the game no longer starts Google's ad software and no longer shows the tracking prompt.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Unity In-App Purchasing and Apple

Apple processes every payment. We never see card or payment details, and the App Store shows real-money prices in your local currency.

To talk to the App Store, the game uses Unity In-App Purchasing, a library by Unity Technologies. Whenever a purchase starts, completes or fails, the library sends Unity a purchase event with the product, price and currency, the transaction ID and Apple's transaction data, the time, and device details: phone model, iOS version, language and region settings, total storage capacity, time since the phone was last started, app version, the identifier for vendors (IDFV), and a random Unity installation ID and session ID. Unity uses these to run, secure and analyse its purchase service. The library is set up so that it does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA). Unity's policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Purchases in the game

  • NO ADS is bought once and belongs to your Apple Account. You can restore it at any time with SHOP → RESTORE PURCHASES or SETTINGS → RESTORE PURCHASES.
  • Teeth packs are consumable. Each one adds a fixed number of teeth to your in-game wallet, and that number is shown before you buy. Teeth never expire, there are no random rewards, and bought teeth are kept only on your phone with the rest of your progress.
  • Anything priced in teeth (heroes, buddies, upgrades and finishes) can also be earned in play. Every purchase is optional.

Your controls

  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions that require it): Google's consent form appears when you first play. To review, change or withdraw your choice later, open SETTINGS on the main menu and tap PRIVACY OPTIONS. The button is there whenever your region requires a consent choice.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency): iOS asks once whether Patakla! may track you. You can change the answer whenever you like in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking or Settings → Patakla! → Allow Tracking. Without permission the advertising identifier stays unused and you are not tracked across other companies' apps and websites. The game plays the same either way.
  • Google ad personalisation: myadcenter.google.com.
  • US state privacy laws (California and others): to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising, leave tracking off and, where it is shown, use SETTINGS → PRIVACY OPTIONS. If Google shows a US state privacy message in the game, you can choose there as well.
  • No full-screen ads at all: buy NO ADS in the SHOP.

Legal grounds, transfers and how long data is kept

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, your consent (given in Google's consent form and in Apple's tracking prompt) is the basis for personalised ads, consent-based ad measurement and access to the advertising identifier. Legitimate interests cover the non-personalised or limited ads that keep the game running, fraud prevention and diagnostics. Processing a purchase is necessary to deliver what you bought. Google and Unity may handle data outside your country, the United States included, relying on safeguards like the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

  • The save file on your phone stays until you delete the app.
  • We keep nothing from the game, so we have nothing to delete. Support e-mails are deleted on request.
  • Google keeps advertising data as described at policies.google.com/technologies/retention; Unity and Apple keep data as their own policies describe.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK and US state laws (the CCPA/CPRA, for example) may give you rights over your personal data, depending on where you live: to see it, have it corrected or deleted, or get a copy you can take elsewhere; to object to or limit its use; to withdraw consent whenever you like, without affecting earlier processing; to opt out of its sale or sharing and of targeted advertising; and to use any of these rights without being treated differently. Your local data protection authority also accepts complaints.

To use these rights for data held by Google or Unity, use their privacy tools linked above, or write to us and we will help you send the request. We never receive data that identifies you, so we cannot look up records ourselves.

Children and the age rating

Patakla! shows cartoon fantasy violence and carries a 13+ age rating on the App Store. It is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone below 13 (or below the minimum age where you live). Ads stay within content rated PG or lower. Screen Time and the iOS privacy settings let parents control purchases and tracking. If a child may have shared personal information, write to us and we will help you ask Google or Unity to delete it.

If you e-mail us

The game sends us nothing, and we have no account system, analytics of our own or servers, so we cannot see or look up individual players. An e-mail gives us your address and your message. We use them only to reply and delete them when you ask.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, this page and its "Last updated" date change with it; important changes are also mentioned in the app's update notes.

Google AdMob (reklamlar) ve Google'ın izin aracı

Reklamlar iki biçimde çıkar:

  • Tam ekran reklamlar, bir bölümün ya da bir Akın Modu oyununun sonuç ekranından sonra, devam ederken çıkabilir. Oyunu hiçbir zaman bölmez; Galata Sahili ve Bebek Sahili'ni (ilk iki bölüm) bitirmeden hiç çıkmaz ve birkaç dakikada en fazla bir kez gelir.
  • Ödüllü reklamlar yalnızca sen istersen oynar: yere serildikten sonra ayağa kalkmak için (her oyunda bir kez) ya da henüz açmadığın bir kahramanla bir oyun oynamak için. Her zaman hayır diyebilirsin. Reklam yüklenemezse (ör. internet yokken) ödülü yine alırsın.

Reklam göstermek, sıklığını sınırlamak, ölçmek, sahtekârlıkla mücadele etmek ve analiz için uygulamadaki Google yazılımı şunları toplar:

  • Cihaz tanımlayıcıları: Apple reklam kimliği (IDFA), yalnızca iOS sorduğunda izlemeye izin verirsen; aksi hâlde bu uygulama ya da geliştiriciyle sınırlı tanımlayıcılar (ör. satıcı tanımlayıcısı IDFV) ve Google'ın uygulamaya özel tanımlayıcıları.
  • IP adresi ve ondan çıkarılan yaklaşık (kaba) konum (ülke ya da bölge gibi). Google'ın izin aracı da bunu, sana hangi gizlilik kurallarının uygulanacağına karar vermek için kullanır. Oyun GPS konumuna hiçbir zaman erişmez.
  • Reklam etkinliği: istenen ve gösterilen reklamlar, reklamlara dokunmalar, sonuna kadar izlenen ödüllü reklamlar ve uygulama açılışları.
  • Cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri: telefon modeli, iOS sürümü, dil ve uygulama sürümü.
  • Tanı verileri: reklam yazılımının çökme ve performans verileri.

Kişiselleştirilmiş reklamları yalnızca gerekli izni verdiysen ve izlemeye izin verdiysen görürsün. Aksi hâlde Google, içeriğe ve yaklaşık konuma göre seçilen kişiselleştirilmemiş ya da sınırlı reklamlar gösterebilir. Oyun, reklamları PG ya da daha düşük dereceli içerikle sınırlar.

REKLAMSIZ sahibiysen tam ekran reklam yoktur; ayağa kalkma ve kahraman deneme reklamsız gelir (yine her oyunda bir kez). Sonraki açılıştan itibaren oyun Google'ın reklam yazılımını başlatmaz ve izleme sorusunu göstermez.
